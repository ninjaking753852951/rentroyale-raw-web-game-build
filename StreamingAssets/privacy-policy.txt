<size=150%><b>Privacy Policy</b></size>

Last Updated: May 2026

Landgrab Royale uses limited gameplay and technical data to provide online multiplayer features, analytics, and game functionality.

<size=125%><b>Information Collected</b></size>

The game may collect or process:

• CrazyGames account username
• Gameplay statistics and session information
• Device and browser technical information
• Local save and preference data
• In-game chat messages (when chat is enabled)

<size=125%><b>Analytics</b></size>

Landgrab Royale uses <b>Unity Analytics</b> to help understand gameplay behavior, improve balance, identify technical issues, and improve the overall player experience.

Unity Analytics may collect technical and gameplay-related information according to Unity’s own privacy policies.

The game includes optional online multiplayer and chat functionality. Messages sent through in-game chat may be temporarily processed or transmitted to other players as part of normal gameplay operation.

Players are responsible for the information they choose to share through chat.

<size=125%><b>Data Usage</b></size>

Collected information is used for:

• Gameplay analytics and balancing
• Saving game preferences

• Moderation and functionality of in-game systems

This game may use third-party services including:
